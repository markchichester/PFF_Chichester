# Stephen's Sunday Dinner

A phone timer and checklist for Sunday dinners, Thanksgiving and Christmas, made for Dad.

Pick a menu, set when dinner should be on the table, and the app works backward to tell you
what to do and when: what goes in the oven, what comes out, and when to put the water on.

## Put it on GitHub Pages
1. Go to github.com and choose **New repository**. Name it something like `sunday-dinner` and make it **Public**.
2. Click **uploading an existing file**, drag in every file from this folder, and click **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under "Build and deployment", set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, then choose **main** and **/ (root)**. Click **Save**.
4. After about a minute, the page shows your link, for example `https://YOURNAME.github.io/sunday-dinner/`.
5. Open the link on your phone and add it to your home screen:
   * iPhone: Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
   * Android: Chrome → ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**.

## Tips
- Keep the app open on screen while you cook. It keeps your phone awake, and phones pause web pages in the background.
- Tap a dish to change its weight, cook time or steps. Every step is set as "minutes before dinner."
- If you're running late, tap **+5** or **+15** and everything that isn't done yet moves with it.
- Use **Save menu** to keep your own versions, like "Dad's Christmas."
- Your menus are saved on your phone, in that browser.
